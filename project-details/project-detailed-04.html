Family Haven Shopify Ecommerce Store

Project Overview:

Family Haven is a UK-focused Shopify pet accessories store project. The work included store setup, product positioning, fulfilment planning, product page improvements, SEO blog structure, ad testing and conversion-focused messaging.

Store & Fulfilment Work:

The store focused on pet accessories with UK-only targeting, free shipping messaging, return policy setup, product collections, CJ dropshipping workflow and customer-friendly content. Product testing included grooming and pet care items such as steam brushes and smart pet dryers.

Marketing Work:

Campaign planning covered Meta, TikTok and Pinterest testing, creative direction, product copy, SEO blog planning, ad angle development and traffic/conversion monitoring. The project involved evaluating checkout behaviour, add-to-cart activity and product-level performance.

Skills Demonstrated:

Shopify setup, product research, ecommerce copywriting, SEO content planning, paid ad strategy, analytics thinking, dropshipping operations, customer policy setup and conversion-led product presentation.

Ecommerce success is built from product selection, trust signals and consistent testing.
